# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Positive behavior includes demonstrating empathy and kindness, respecting
different viewpoints, giving and accepting constructive feedback, accepting
responsibility and apologizing for mistakes, and focusing on what is best for
the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or attention, trolling,
insults, personal or political attacks, public or private harassment,
publishing private information without permission, and other conduct that
would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers clarify and enforce acceptable behavior and take fair,
appropriate corrective action in response to conduct they consider
inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful. Maintainers may remove,
edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban a
contributor. They will explain moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code applies in project spaces and when an individual officially
represents the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately through
the repository host's maintainer contact channel. Reports are reviewed
promptly, fairly, and confidentially. Maintainers must respect the privacy and
security of reporters.

Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant Community Impact Guidelines:
correction, warning, temporary ban, and permanent ban, proportionate to impact
and repeated behavior.
