# Scope and Authorization

Authorization applies to the exact asset, identity, technique, time window, and effect. Record the user-stated authorization boundary and clarify omissions or contradictions; do not infer it from traffic. A selected Caido scope is a technical guard, not legal authorization. For active work, require both explicit authorization and an allowed `caido_is_in_scope` result. Deny/ambiguous/missing rules stop the action. Do not override Unicode, IP, port, scheme, or deny-rule decisions manually.
